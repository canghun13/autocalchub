How we build these

No black boxes. If a number matters to your decision, you should be able to see where it came from.

The math is explained

Calculators explain the formula they use and the assumptions behind the result, on the same page.

Sources are linked

Figures come from public sources such as AAA, Experian, NAIC, Freddie Mac, Fannie Mae and the IRS, listed and linked on the pages that use them.

Checked before it goes live

New calculators are tested against a separately written version of the same math across several scenarios.

Updated and corrected

Prices and rates are refreshed as new data comes out, and corrections are listed publicly.
